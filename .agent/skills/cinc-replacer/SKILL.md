# CINC Pro Replacement Strategy

Based on deep competitive research (2024), here is the feature parity map we must execute to build a "multi-billion dollar" dashboard.

## 1. The Core Engine: "Intelligent Pipeline"
**CINC Feature:** Auto-lead routing, "Pond" accounts for unresponsive leads, stage-based pipelines.
**Our Superiority:** 
- **Behavioral Scoring:** Instead of just "New" or "Contacted", we rank by `score` (0-100) derived from real actions.
- **AI Daily Briefing:** CINC just shows a list. We generate a briefing: "Call Maria, she looked at 5 homes today."

## 2. Automation & Nurture
**CINC Features:** 
- "CINC AI" ($200/mo extra) -> 24/7 SMS qualification.
- "AutoTracks" -> Drip campaigns based on triggers.
- Behavioral Messaging -> Texts sent moments after website activity.

**Our Replacement:**
- **Standard AI SMS:** Included free (via Claude/Twilio). No "add-on" fees.
- **Smart Triggers:** We trigger not just on "signup" but on "price drop on favorited home" or "score crossed 70".
- **Contextual Drips:** Our drips read the lead's last viewed property to customize the message.

## 3. The Dashboard Experience (The "Profound" Part)
To beat CINC visually and functionally:
- **Zero Clutter:** CINC is dense and tabular. We use "Compass-style" luxury minimalism.
- **Dark Mode:** Gold on Black. CINC is standard SaaS white/gray.
- **Speed:** Instant page loads (Next.js) vs legacy CRM loading times.
- **Mobile First:** CINC has an app, but our *web dashboard* must work perfectly on mobile web for the agent.

## Implementation Checklist
- [ ] **Smart Lead Pipeline:** Kanban view sorted by Score (Hot/Warm/Cool).
- [ ] **AI Command Center:** The "Launchpad" equivalent, but with generative briefings.
- [ ] **Transparent Scoring:** Click a score to see EXACTLY why it went up (CINC is a black box).
